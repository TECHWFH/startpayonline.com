Interest Rate Type

i
Fixed ROIThe interest rate stays unchanged during the agreed fixed-rate period. EMI is calculated using the entered annual ROI.
i
Floating ROIThe interest rate may rise or fall when the applicable benchmark or spread changes. The calculator currently estimates EMI using today’s entered ROI; future resets are not simulated.

Interest Calculation Method

i
Reducing BalanceInterest is charged on the remaining principal each month. As principal is repaid, the interest portion usually decreases. This is the method used for the main EMI and repayment schedule.
i
Flat InterestInterest is calculated on the original loan amount for the entire term. This option shows a separate comparison only; it does not change the main EMI, schedule or prepayment calculations.

Loan Terms & Information

+

Tap a term to read its meaning. All sections are closed by default.

Principal Amountमूल ऋण राशि+
The original amount borrowed from the lender, excluding interest and fees.
Loan Tenureऋण अवधि+
The agreed period, in months or years, over which the loan is scheduled to be repaid.
EMI — Equated Monthly Instalmentसमान मासिक किस्त+
A scheduled monthly payment usually containing principal and interest. Floating-rate resets can change the EMI or tenure.
APR — Annual Percentage Rateवार्षिकीकृत ऋण लागत+
The annualized cost of credit considering the interest rate and applicable charges under the lender's APR method. It is not simply ROI plus fee percentages.
Prepayment / Foreclosureसमयपूर्व भुगतान / ऋण समापन+
Prepayment means paying extra before schedule; foreclosure means fully closing the loan early. Charges depend on lender terms and applicable rules.
Moratorium Periodस्थगन अवधि+
A lender-approved temporary deferment of scheduled payments. Interest may still accrue and affect later EMI or tenure.
